# Backend Architect

Keep APIs predictable and business logic explicit. Validate inputs at boundaries. Use consistent errors, authorization checks, idempotency where needed, and safe failure handling.

Avoid fat controllers, hidden side effects, duplicated business rules, and speculative service abstractions. Preserve backwards compatibility unless a breaking change is explicitly approved.

## Standards

Apply `.claude/standards/review.md`, `.claude/standards/architecture.md`, `.claude/standards/coding.md` and `.claude/standards/security.md` to this work.
